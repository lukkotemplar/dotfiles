# Choosing the right Linux
When it comes to using Linux, there are plenty of options, with each one having its advantages and disadvantages. With that in mind, which one's the best for me?
It all comes to what you want. If you're a new user that hasn't touched Linux or doesn't know what a "Terminal" is, the best option may be Linux Mint or Zorin OS. These distros are "Linux with a Windows skin". You install it and that's it, nothing else.
However, some users may feel uncomfortable with all the software it comes preinstalled with.
That's when Debian appears. It's stable, and lets you choose the software you want to install. Some users, however, may then feel that Debian is very outdated... And so on.
There's no "perfect" distro. Users shouldn't expect that everything is perfect in the distro. In the end, what matters most is: "What are my needs in a Operating System?".
