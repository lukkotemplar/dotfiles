# Cádiz
The commuter network of Cádiz consists of 2 cercanías lines (C1 and C1-a) and a tram line (T1)
## COMMUTER
### C1
The stations are: Cádiz, San Severiano, Segunda Aguada, Estadio, Cortadura, Río Arillo, San Fernando-Bahía Sur, San Fernando-Centro, Puerto Real, Las Aletas, Valdelagrana, Puerto de Santa María, Jerez de la Frontera and Aeropuerto de Jerez
### C1-a
The stations are: Las Aletas and Universidad
